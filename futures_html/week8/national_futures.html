2026-27 College Football Playoff Odds

Major Moves

LATEST UPDATE: Ohio State (6-1) opened as the favorite in January and stayed there until its Week 7 game at Indiana. The Buckeyes won 19-15, but QB Julian Sayin suffered an arm/shoulder injury, and his return this season is uncertain.

Meanwhile, Notre Dame (6-1) lost to BYU 28-24 on the road. But the game ended in controversy, with a noncall on what appeared to be pass interference. Regardless, there still should've been 1 second on the clock for the Irish to run another play, from inside BYU's 10-yard line.

Oddsmakers apparently counted the Sayin injury and the Notre Dame-BYU finish in favor of the Fighting Irish. Another reason oddsmakers leaned into Notre Dame: No. 1 Georgia, No. 5 Alabama and No. 7 Texas also lost, and as noted above, so did No. 5 Indiana.

So Notre Dame is now the +400 favorite 2026 CFP futures odds, in from +500 before Week 7. The Fighting Irish are No. 1 in ticket count and No. 2 in money at BetMGM.

"Notre Dame remains one of our biggest liabilities at the moment," BetMGM trading manager Seamus Magee said, while noting there's two opinions on the Irish. "We've seen some Notre Dame buyers.

"But we've seen a lot of action on Notre Dame to miss the playoffs, as well, going from +600 to +450 in the last week."

Ohio State slipped from the +400 fave to the +500 second choice, joined by Georgia (6-1), which went from +450 to +500.

Despite Sayin's injury and Ohio State slightly slipping in College Football Playoff championship odds, Buckeyes wideout Jeremiah Smith remains the favorite in Heisman Trophy odds.

Rounding out the top five in BetMGM's 2026 College Football Playoff odds:

  • Miami (6-0) +600
  • Indiana (6-1) +1000

"We're still seeing a lot of Miami love," Magee said. "It's pretty wide open right now, though. Even Ohio State, with the QB uncertainty, isn't a slam dunk to make the CFP."

Tickets, Money, Liability

Miami's climb up the CFP futures odds board continues, albeit barely. The Hurricanes nearly caught the same upset bug as many of their top-10 brethren in Week 7.

Miami trailed twice in the second half, but ultimately scratched out a 43-35 double-overtime victory vs. Florida State. So the Hurricanes' odds rightly improved, from +650 to the aforementioned +600.

Miami took a big wager in CFP futures odds earlier in the season: $20,000 at +1100, for a potential profit of $220,000.

Ole Miss (5-1) came away from Week 7 looking better, as well. The Rebels blasted Missouri 31-3 and subsequently improved from +2000 to +1700 in odds to win the CFP.

A BetMGM customer put $5,000 on Ole Miss +3500 to win the national title. If the Rebels make that run, the bettor will win $175,000.

"Ohio State, Georgia and Indiana remain three of our best results, so we're pulling for them," BetMGM trading manager Seamus Magee said. "Miami is about break-even right now. It will be interesting to see what happens after Miami plays Notre Dame."

And the Longhorns, despite their two-game losing skid, are the liability leader at BetMGM. Texas (4-2) dropped from +700 to +1300 in 2026-27 College Football Playoff futures.

"Texas is still a liability, but action has cooled a lot in the last three weeks," Magee said. "Alabama had a moment where we were writing a lot of action, but even the Tide have seen it cool down the last two weeks.

In order, BetMGM noted its largest liabilities in college football national championship futures are:

  • Texas
  • Notre Dame
  • Oregon

LSU (5-2) rebounded from an upset loss at Kentucky to blank Mississippi State 31-0 at home. So after tumbling from +1200 to +3000, the Tigers rebounded to +2000.

"LSU is about break even for us. The Tigers' schedule ramps up in November, so I imagine that's when we'll see the most action," Magee said.

Indeed, on back-to-back-to-back weeks, LSU is home vs. Alabama, home vs. Texas, then at Tennessee.

BYU (6-0) is getting a little attention after the upset of visiting Notre Dame. The Cougars leapt from +10000 (100/1) to +4000.

"We've definitely seen an uptick in action on BYU off the upset. But we're in a great spot with the Cougars, a solid winner for us at the moment," Magee said.

Magee also pointed to a potential under-the-radar play: USC (6-1), which is +8000.

"I think it's hard to find value outside of the top 10 teams or so," he said. "If you're looking for a long shot I think USC could be worth a flier. The Trojans are more than likely going to face a backup QB at home against Ohio State [on Oct. 31], which would really bolster the resume.

"They obviously have a tough game at Indiana, but they would be coming off a bye. If they win one of those two games and beat UCLA in the last game of the season, they'll have a great argument to get into the CFP."

College Football Playoff Format

The College Football Playoff is heading toward its third season with the 12-team format.

The field of 12 is comprised of the five highest-ranked conference champions, and the next seven highest-ranked other teams. The rankings will again be determined by the College Football Playoff committee.

How To Bet On College Football Futures

American football is the most popular game among bettors in the United States, and a large portion of that interest is from college football. Analyzing futures odds and predicting which school will become the champion every season continues to receive attention at the betting counter.

Check out our list of the best sportsbooks and sportsbook promos to find the best and most secure places to make college football bets, as well as exclusive sportsbook offers. You can also use our Caesars sportsbook promo code to unlock potential bonus bets.

College Football Futures can be bet on throughout the year. As with any betting market, there are wagering peaks and valleys. After each season ends in January, oddsmakers send out their opening futures for the following season.

The main market is for the NCAA Division 1 Football Bowl Subdivision. There are more than 125 teams divided into 10 conferences, and a group of independent schools, including Notre Dame.

Some sportsbooks set college football futures on every school eligible to win the championship. But other operators keep the list at 50 teams or even fewer, since some lower-tier schools don’t have a competitive chance. The Future Odds market doesn’t receive as much attention in the spring, but interest tends to pick up in the summer and in the first couple weeks of the regular season.

Interest tends to heighten again as conference championship weekend draws near. And betting on College Football Playoff futures odds really picks up after the 12-team field is set.

Strictly Necessary Cookies

Always Active

These cookies are necessary for the website to function and cannot be switched off in our systems. They are usually only set in response to actions made by you which amount to a request for services, such as setting your privacy preferences, logging in or filling in forms. You can set your browser to block or alert you about these cookies, but some parts of the site will not then work. These cookies do not store any personally identifiable information.

Targeting Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used by those companies to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant adverts on other sites. They do not store directly personal information, but are based on uniquely identifying your browser and internet device. If you do not allow these cookies, you will experience less targeted advertising.

Performance Cookies

These cookies allow us to count visits and traffic sources so we can measure and improve the performance of our site. They help us to know which pages are the most and least popular and see how visitors move around the site. All information these cookies collect is aggregated and therefore anonymous. If you do not allow these cookies we will not know when you have visited our site, and will not be able to monitor its performance.
