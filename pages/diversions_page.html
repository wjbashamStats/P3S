A flagged (🔥) row's Spread Diff / Total Diff cell names the actual side we'd bet -- the team and number for spread, Over/Under and the number for total -- not just the raw point gap, so you don't have to work out which direction a positive or negative diff favors. Predicted spread = SP+ differential + home-field edge (team_ratings_2025.csv's SP and HFACW columns) — SP+ is opponent-adjusted, which is why it's used for spread instead of each team's raw scoring average. Predicted total = sum of each team's own "Team Total" (expected points) rating. Both are simple, un-fitted power-rating comparisons, not a tuned model, and there's no backtest behind this specific page — treat the diffs as directional, not gospel. The five-factor radar, power table, and QB comparison in each preview are supporting context for your own judgment, not additional inputs folded into the predicted number. Radar axes plot rank (1 = best nationally) with a linear scale, outer edge = best. Power-table ranks are shaded on the same scale (green = good, orange = weak) relative to the full 136-team FBS field. QB stats are each starter's real 2025 season — PFF grade may be blank for a player PFF didn't grade enough snaps to rate, or a true freshman with no 2025 record. This is analysis, not betting advice.
