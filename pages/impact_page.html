Stats and usage share are 2025 season-to-date -- the most recent complete season, and the baseline this project projects a new week from. Team shown is each player's current (2026) roster where known via the transfer portal -- a gold "'25 at [team]" note marks anyone whose 2025 production happened somewhere else. Primary rank/sort is each player's PFF overall grade within position (national and conference) -- only players PFF actually graded that season get a grade rank; a separate production rank (by primary counting stat) is shown in the player drawer. Advanced metrics (aDOT, big-time throws/turnover-worthy plays for QBs, yards after contact for backs, yards per route run for receivers, etc.) come from PFF's own season export, joined by PFF's player ID. Cell shading in the season table is a percentile within position. "This Week" is whatever week/season is shown in the header tag above -- coverage depends on how many games had a posted prop when that data was pulled.
